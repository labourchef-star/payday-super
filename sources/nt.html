NT public and regional holidays

Public holidays for the Northern Territory (NT) are under the Public Holidays Act 1981 and apply to all businesses across the NT.

There are also regional holidays which only apply to regions celebrating their annual show days.

For information about your responsibilities, rights and entitlements during public holidays, go to the Australian Government's Fair Work Ombudsman website.
