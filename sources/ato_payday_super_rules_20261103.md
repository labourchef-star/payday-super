1. Payment deadlines for Payday Super
https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/payday-super/paying-super-on-payday/payment-deadlines-for-payday-super
- "Your super guarantee contribution is on time if it is received by your employee's super fund (with all the necessary information to allocate the contribution to the employee's member account) within 7 business days after paying your employee."
- "This information is only for employee earnings paid from 1 July 2026." Earlier earnings: quarterly rules.
- Business day = any day other than a Saturday or Sunday, or "a day that is a public holiday for the whole of any Australian state or territory" ... "even if you are not in that state or territory". Part-state holidays (e.g. Royal Hobart Show Day) are still business days.
- First contribution for a new employee / new fund: "within 20 business days after the relevant QE day".
- Worked examples: QE 9 Jul 2026 first contribution due 7 Aug 2026 (20 BD); QE 30 Jul 2026 due 11 Aug 2026 (7 BD); both include an extra day for NT Picnic Day.
